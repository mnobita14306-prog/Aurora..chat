Privacy Policy
🔒

Your privacy matters

How Aurora handles your chats, profile, and device data.

1. Overview

Aurora is a messaging app that can run locally on your device and optionally sync through its Supabase cloud when available. This policy explains what is stored and how it is used.

2. Data we store

  • Account: username, display name, password (local registry / cloud account), profile photo, bio, phone, email.
  • Messages: chat text, media you send, timestamps, read/delivery markers.
  • Preferences: theme, notifications, mute, blocked users, recovery/backup settings.
  • Presence: online/last-seen style activity for contacts.

3. Where data lives

  • This device: browser localStorage for local accounts, chats, and settings.
  • Cloud (optional): Supabase — accounts, database and media — when the cloud is connected.

If cloud is off, your chats stay on the device only.

4. How we use data

  • Deliver messages and calls between participants.
  • Show profile details you choose to save.
  • Improve security (password change, recovery codes, session controls).
  • Remember your app preferences.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Message content is only available to chat participants and infrastructure needed to deliver the service (e.g. Supabase when enabled).

6. Your controls

  • Edit or clear profile fields anytime.
  • Mute, block, clear, or delete conversations.
  • Sign out to end the current session on this device.
  • Use recovery email / backup codes for account recovery.

7. Security

Transport uses HTTPS when cloud features are on. Keep your password private, enable recovery options, and avoid shared devices without signing out.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Use Settings → Help & Feedback or email [email protected].
